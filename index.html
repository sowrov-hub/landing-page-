Before we started, GV Roof Repairs didn't have a Google Business Profile at all — and no website either. I built both from scratch: set up the profile the right way, got a real website live, added the photos, the services, everything Google needed to actually trust the business. This is that same profile today — 5.0 stars across 13 reviews, fully set up and running.

The ranking

Before, searching "roof repair Shepparton" wouldn't have shown GV Roof Repairs anywhere. Once the profile and local SEO work were in place, they climbed straight to the top of the map — the first result homeowners in the area actually see.

The calls

A profile Google trusts is a profile Google puts in front of people. This is what that looked like in real numbers — 73 calls booked straight from the Business Profile, tracked by Google itself.

Not everyone picks up the phone straight away — some just want to send a quick message first. That's a channel GV Roof Repairs didn't really have before. Now it brings in leads on its own, 16 message enquiries and counting.

The website used to just sit there. Once it was actually findable on Google, it started pulling its weight — 28 leads through the contact form, with organic Google search as the single biggest source.
